POLINA ZINZIVER

Multimedia artist working with CGI, VR sculpting, a/v live performance and mediated installation.

I work with the body as it is edited by others — by the state, by tradition, by platforms and their filters.

I once spent two months in a mountain village with no mirrors, where women’s knees and shoulders had to stay covered. Back online, I had to blur the nipples of my own digital bodies to keep them visible. These are different regimes of moderation, but they cut the same body map.

2050இ

a/v live/ VR performance
2025

2050இ is an a/v live and VR performance about the body as an interface, with music by Symphocat.

The artist performs wearing a VR headset. Every gesture and movement is turned into images in real time and spreads across large-scale screens around the audience. The body is at once the author of the picture and the device that produces it.

Nothing is illustrated and no story is told: the performer draws the landscape the audience is standing in, and is exposed by it at the same time — watched, projected, translated into data. 2050இ treats virtual space not as neutral ground but as a place where vulnerability and revolt are still possible.

SACRED HYPER RACEgame level design, 2024

A race map for Fortnite, made with MultiGamesStudio. Inspired by South Asian music and medieval European alchemy, the track is a surreal journey through the subconscious: from the Sun Man’s gateway of transformation, through a labyrinth of self-doubt and monstrous thresholds, into a cathartic rainforest — and on to an ecstatic finish.

THERMOSPHERE

a/v live
2023

An immersive a/v live performance made with musician Symphocat about the atmosphere of control and censorship in Russia in 2023. Performed on the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre, St Petersburg.

AI is used here as a way around restrictions: familiar social and cultural images are abstracted until they can no longer be read — or banned — directly.

The visuals react to the sound and follow the logic of granular synthesis: material is cut into fragments and reassembled into a new, shifting landscape of resistance and transformation.

Hold me, hold me, hold

performance
2022

Collaboration with choreographer Alina Belyagina.

Two dancers, moving images and sound mix the electronic textures of a video game with church vocals and house music. The real and the imaginary swap places, and the imaginary becomes something you can touch. Bodies stop being clearly human, turn into monstrous, animal-like creatures and grow into the devices on stage — a microphone, a fog machine.

THIRD PERSON
REWIRING

mediated installation, mixed media (prototype)
2023

An installation about how the state and other controlling structures reach the body directly, on a physical level.

The viewer sits in a chair designed to be uncomfortable and sees themselves from the side — like an out-of-body experience, or the third-person view in a video game. Meanwhile a hypnotic voice “overwrites” them, insisting that they are an NPC: a non-playable character, programmed for utilitarian, repetitive actions.

Work in progress; currently exists as a 3D visualisation.

FACE POSITIVITY

series of 3D video portraits
2022

A series of 3D video portraits of digital characters with unconventional beauty.

Beauty standards now apply not only to bodies but to faces, and digital tools — filters, retouching, 3D — mostly push those standards further, towards the unreal.

Face Positivity uses the same tools the other way round: to build the faces a filter would “correct”, and to leave them as they are.

NUMBERS

a/v performance
2021

Audiovisual performance with composer Vladislav Grigoryev, the electronic duo reboot, a choir and musicians of the St Petersburg Conservatory. Presented on the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre on 16 March 2021 as part of the Classic Electric project.

Through music and image, Numbers explores the collective unconscious that opens up in states of delirium and insomnia.

The visuals were sculpted in VR with the “expressive VR modeling” technique: forms grow directly out of hand movements in space.

YOU ARE ENOUGH

video art, full-dome projection
2021

Immersive video art for full-dome projection, with sound by Valeria Polyanskaya, inspired by meditative states. The journey goes outward — beyond the planet, the universe, the galaxy — and at the same time inward, deep into the subconscious, towards the higher self.

The central sculpture was made with the “expressive VR modeling” technique, inspired by the music of Jóhann Jóhannsson and Eduard Artemyev.

INTER_PHASE

VR installation
2019

A VR journey to regain control over technology and information, and to find a balance between them and the meditative self.

Instead of escaping outward, away from technology, Inter_phase proposes an escape inward: learning to live with technology the way we live with our non-digital relationships — weighing what hurts against what helps.

An AR face filter, inter_phase_v1, extended the installation onto the phone screen, making visible the invisible interface that ties us to our smartphones — our new extremities.

B PRESSURE

video art
2019

Video art made for the Digi_Gal exhibition “Interactive Identities” in Berlin.

A work about the mental and physical pressure of beauty standards, imposed through the media by a patriarchal society.
