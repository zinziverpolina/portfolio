POLINA ZINZIVER

Multimedia artist who works in various media including CGI, mediated installations and a/v performances.

Polina highlights themes of state control over bodies through media and biopolitics, creating sexuality and physicality in real and digital spaces, free from traditional ideas and standards.

Their artistic practice is aimed at finding ways of interaction through care and non-violence.

2050இ

a/v live/ VR performance
2025

2050இ is poetic a/v live, transmedia performance that weaves together real-time audiovisual composition, VR technology, and poetic embodiment to interrogate the politics of the body in the digital age. At its core, the work explores the tension and transference between physical presence and virtual image, positioning the artist not only as a performer but as a conduit—an embodied transmitter of algorithmic vision.

Through the use of a VR headset, the artist’s gestures and movement generate dynamic visual content that unfolds in real time across large-scale digital screens. This mediated choreography dissolves the boundary between creator and projection, foregrounding the artist’s body as both subject and interface—an ephemeral locus where data and desire, flesh and code converge.

Rather than illustrating a narrative, 2050இ becomes a living landscape—fluid, confrontational, and intimate. By situating digital tools within a poetic and somatic framework, the performance disrupts the presumed neutrality of virtual space, transforming it into a site of vulnerability and revolt.

THERMOSPHERE

a/v live
2023

Thermosphere is an immersive audiovisual live performance, developed in collaboration with musician Symphocat, that distills the pervasive atmosphere of control and censorship in Russia, 2023. Performed on a new stage of Aleksandriyskiy Theatre, Saint-Petersburg.

Employing ai as a means of subversion, the work abstracts and reconfigures socio-cultural visual codes, circumventing imposed restrictions.

The synthesis of reactive visuals and sound mirrors granular synthesis techniques — disassembling, fragmenting, and reconstituting elements into a new, fluid landscape of resistance and transformation.

Hold me, hold me, hold

performance
2022

Collaboration with choreographer Alina Belyagina.

Merging together the real and the imaginary, two dancers along with moving images and soundscape, amalgamate the electronic frequencies of the reality of a videogame with church vocals and house music to create a space for a transgressive experience that holds the potentiality for becoming imaginary, making the imaginary palpable. Bodies indefinable as either human transfigure into monstrous animalistic beasts extending into technological devices like a microphone and a fog machine.

THIRD PERSON
REWIRING

mediated installation, mixed media (prototype)
2023

The installation is aimed at revealing the presence of the state and other controlling structures in our lives at the physical level.

It provides viewers with both a bodily experience evoked by sitting in a chair designed to cause discomfort to the sitter, and offers the possibility of an out-of-body experience, allowing one to observe oneself from the side, similar to the out-of-body experience in the astral dimension or “third person view” in the virtual dimension. While listening to a hypnotic text that "overwrites" and convinces them that they are NPCs.

Non-Playable Character (NPC) is a term that refers to a computer game character designed to perform utilitarian functions and pre-programmed for repetitive actions.

The installation is a work in progress and exists only as a 3D visualisation at the moment.

FACE POSITIVITY

series of 3d portraits
2022

A series of video portraits dedicated to unconventional beauty in virtual spaces.

Today, not only our body, but also our faces are subject to beauty stereotypes, and digital tools are mainly used to enhance facial beauty patterns and standards to an unrealistic and unnatural degree.

“Face Positivity” is a protest and an attempt to break out of this pattern and celebrate the unique.

NUMBERS

a/v performance
2021

Audiovisual performance in collaboration with composer Vladislav Grigoriev, the electronic duo reboot and musicians from the St. Petersburg Conservatory for a performance on the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre, St. Petersburg, as part of the Classic Electric project.

The work of figures, through musical and visual images, explores the collective unconscious we access through a state of delirium and insomnia.

The visual accompaniment was created using the “expressive VR modeling” technique.

YOU ARE ENOUGH

video art, full-dome projection
2021

Immersive video art with sound by Valeria Polyanskaya inspired by meditative states. Rising up beyond the planet, universe, galaxy and at the same time deep into your subconscious to connect with your higher self.

The sculpture underlying the work is made using the “expressive VR modeling” technique, inspired by the music of composers Johan Johanson and Eduard Artemyev.

INTER_PHASE

VR installation
2019

The artwork depicts a journey to regain control of technology and information and our search for inner balance between them and the meditative self.

Inter_phase hopes to offer a solution by not escaping outwards from these negative and positive aspects, but an escape inward to find a way to embrace technology by balancing the negative and positive aspects of them into our lives as we would with our non-digital relationships.
