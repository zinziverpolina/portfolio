2050இ

a/v live/ VR performance
2025

Conceived as a small, intimate performance about bodily trauma — body horror, drama — 2050இ grew into a large-scale one. An intimate wound blown up to the size of a screen stops being private.

Music by Symphocat. I perform in a VR headset: every gesture becomes an image in real time on large screens around the audience. I draw the landscape the audience stands in, and stay exposed inside it — watched, projected, turned into data. Russia, 2025.

THERMOSPHERE

a/v live
2023

An immersive a/v live performance made with musician Symphocat about the atmosphere of control and censorship in Russia in 2023. Performed on the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre, St Petersburg.

AI is used here as a way around restrictions: familiar social and cultural images are abstracted until they can no longer be read — or banned — directly.

Hold me, hold me, hold

performance
2022

Choreography: Alina Belyagina. Visuals and video: Polina Zinziver (media and video artist). Music: Symphocat. Dancers: Maya Selezneva, Veronika Tikhonova.

Two dancers, moving images and sound mix the electronic textures of a video game with church vocals and house music. The real and the imaginary swap places, and the imaginary becomes something you can touch. Bodies stop being clearly human, turn into monstrous, animal-like creatures and grow into the devices on stage — a microphone, a fog machine.

THIRD PERSON
REWIRING

mediated installation, mixed media (prototype)
2023

A work about how the state and other structures of control reach the body directly, bypassing thought.

A chair made to be uncomfortable. You sit down and see yourself from the side on a screen — like leaving your body, or like a game character in third-person view. A calm, hypnotic voice keeps overwriting you: you are an NPC, a non-playable character, made for useful, repetitive actions.

Prototype; exists as a 3D visualisation.

FACE POSITIVITY

series of 3D video portraits
2022

A series of 3D video portraits of digital characters with unconventional beauty.

Beauty standards now apply not only to bodies but to faces, and digital tools — filters, retouching, 3D — mostly push those standards further, towards the unreal. A filter is a machine for the standard face: it measures every face against a norm.

Face Positivity uses the same tools the other way round: to build the faces a filter would “correct”, and to leave them as they are. In close-up the face stops being a profile picture and becomes pure affect.

NUMBERS

a/v performance
2021

Audiovisual performance with composer Vladislav Grigoryev, the electronic duo reboot, a choir and musicians of the St Petersburg Conservatory. Presented on the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre on 16 March 2021 as part of the Classic Electric project.

Through music and image, Numbers explores the collective unconscious that opens up in states of delirium and insomnia.

The visuals were sculpted in VR with the “expressive VR modeling” technique: forms grow directly out of hand movements in space.
