SACRED HYPER RACE

a race map for Fortnite

Role
Art direction
Level design
Unreal assembly
Tools

Studio

A track for Fortnite’s Rocket Racing, built in one month. Three laps through violet organic forms, past creatures, to a temple at the finish. Idea and execution by Polina Zinziver, from the first sketch to the assembled map.

Play in Fortnite → Walk the map ↓ Minigame →

01

Track, creatures & production

The route started from the plan of a real kart circuit; the creatures started as ink drawings in a notebook. Each model was sculpted in Forger, made game-ready in Cinema 4D (retopology and UVs), given a first layer of texture in Procreate — hand-drawn symmetrical patterns — and finished in Adobe Substance 3D Painter. Twelve models for the map; drag to rotate.

NINA RICCI

animations for the Summer 22 collection

Role

Animation
Render
Project
Summer 22 collection
Credits
Art direction by Javier Sola
Year

For the Summer 22 collection: a logo animation and a series of short films built around the house’s symbols and the season’s pieces — the apple, the dove and the Three Graces.

Each film gives one symbol a material of its own — glossy, inflated, liquid. Shown on the brand’s website and social media, in vertical and horizontal versions.
