SACRED HYPER RACE

Fortnite race map: art direction, level design, Unreal assembly
2024

A race track for Fortnite’s Rocket Racing, built in one month. Idea and execution by Polina Zinziver: three laps through violet organic forms, past creatures, to a temple at the finish.

made with MultiGamesStudio

01 · TRACK AND CREATURES

process
April 2024

The route started from the plan of a real kart circuit. The creatures started as ink drawings in a notebook.

02 · SCULPTING

process
April 2024

The creatures were sculpted in Forger, then made game-ready in Cinema 4D: retopology and UVs, twelve models for the map.

03 · SURFACES

process
May 2024

Hand-drawn symmetrical patterns became the textures and decals, painted onto the models in Substance Painter.

04 · ASSEMBLY

process
May 2024

Everything was placed, lit and tuned along the track in Unreal Editor for Fortnite.

RESULT

playable map in Fortnite
June 2024

Published in Rocket Racing: a three-lap race of about three and a half minutes, recorded in live races in June 2024.
