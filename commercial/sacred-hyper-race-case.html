Sacred Hyper Race

a race map for Fortnite, 2024

A track for Fortnite’s Rocket Racing, built in one month. Three laps through violet organic forms, past creatures, to a temple at the finish. Idea and execution by Polina Zinziver, from the first sketch to the assembled map.

Tools
Forger, Cinema 4D, Substance Painter, Unreal Editor for Fortnite
01

Track and creatures

The route started from the plan of a real kart circuit. The creatures started as ink drawings in a notebook.

02

Sculpting

The creatures were sculpted in Forger, then made game-ready in Cinema 4D: retopology and UVs, twelve models for the map.

03

Surfaces

Hand-drawn symmetrical patterns became the textures and decals, painted onto the models in Substance Painter.

04

Assembly

Everything was placed, lit and tuned along the track in Unreal Editor for Fortnite.

05

Result

Published in Rocket Racing: a three-lap race of about three and a half minutes. Recorded in live races, June 2024.

06

The models

All twelve low-poly models from the map. Drag to rotate.
