Contrapoint for Contradiction

(Drone/Ison)
spatial sound, SuperCollider, binaural
work in progress, 2026

In music, counterpoint is two independent melodies sounding at once: each follows its own logic and does not dissolve into the other, and together they make one fabric with tension inside. Here the two lines are the body in the space of control — the state, platforms, moderation, borders (part 1) — and the body in the space of ritual — resonance, community, shelter (part 2). The work neither chooses between them nor reconciles them: the contradiction is not resolved, it becomes form.
